# Wavemax Project Brief

## Status

- Revision: 1
- Approval: DRAFT
- Lifecycle: DISCOVERY

## Problem and outcome

Wavemax currently receives operating information from separate laundromat systems. The product will give the store owner a trustworthy view of operations, workforce, sales, and finance while giving managers only the operational information and direct-report controls required for their work.

The product succeeds only when every displayed result is supported by real source data, a documented calculation, record-level drill-down, and role-correct access.

## Users

### Owner

Needs the complete store view, including operations, order detail, analytics, finance, workforce, approvals, calculation explanations, data quality, and exports.

### Manager

Needs Command Center, Orders, and Workforce Management. The manager may see order value and payment status but may view and approve workforce records only for direct reports. Analytics and Finance are prohibited.

## Product character

- A laundromat command and intelligence product, not a general CRM.
- Omrix-inspired visual quality without Omrix marketing content.
- LaundryOps-AI-inspired order and workforce interactions without its seeded data or unsupported AI claims.
- Colerain-inspired analytical clarity, hover details, filters, methodology, and reconciliation.

## Scope

### In scope

- One store.
- Owner and manager roles.
- Command Center, Orders, Business Analytics/Finance, and Workforce Management.
- WDF POS and Laundro Portal ingestion.
- Data-quality reporting, lineage, drill-down, calculation explanation, and scoped export.

### Out of scope

- Multi-store workflows.
- Recommendations page and AI-generated business results.
- Demonstration datasets in the application.

## Constraints

- Customer, employee, card/account, timesheet, and financial information is sensitive.
- Some source exports may be incomplete or structurally unreliable.
- Original files and records must never be silently changed or removed.
- No implementation begins without an approved phase.
