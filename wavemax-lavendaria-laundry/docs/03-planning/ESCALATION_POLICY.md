# Escalation Policy

Stop and ask the owner when:

- No applicable phase is explicitly approved.
- Work falls outside approved scope.
- Requirements, source definitions, tests, or data disagree.
- A database schema, public API, role boundary, deployment, cost, or retention policy must materially change.
- Data would be deleted, overwritten, irreversibly merged, or sent outside the approved environment.
- Personal, financial, employee, account/card, secret, or credential exposure is possible.
- A metric cannot be reconciled to its approved definition or source control.
- Three evidence-driven repair attempts fail.

## Escalation report

- Task or bug
- Intended outcome
- Current evidence
- Attempts and results
- Root-cause hypotheses
- Decision required
- Options and trade-offs
- Recommended option
- Consequence of waiting
